## Evidence and limits

The normalized original GLB supplies physical materials and measured feature bounds. Capsule approaches, cue-space triangles, sampled visibility, landscape projection and populated cue envelopes are checked. A finite sample of rays or pixels is not proof of zero occlusion at every pixel. Final continuous route and projection evidence must correspond to the current source version.

The final route has13legs and an approximately298m path. Actual CPU controller replay used one initial spawn, no mid-route teleport, and108seconds at60Hz. The support probe repair preserves the0.34m step-height limit; high steps and low ceilings stay blocked.

Reference images are Blender geometry inspections, not browser screenshots. Portrait runtime preserves a real minimum4:3 canvas. Actual browser rendering, texture decoding, mobile controls, downloads and learning effectiveness are not certified.
